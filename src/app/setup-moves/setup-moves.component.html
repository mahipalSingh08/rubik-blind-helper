🔄 Setup Moves Reference

Quick cheat sheet for bringing targets to RFD (Corners) or UB (Edges)

Corners (OP - Target: RFD)

LetterTargetSetupUndo
BURBR D'D R'
CUFLFF'
DURFF R'R F'
FLFUF2F2
GLDFD2 F'F D2
IFLUF' DD' F
............
Showing a sample of common setups. The rule of thumb is: use F, R, D to move the piece to RFD without touching the UBL buffer.

Edges (M2 - Target: UB)

LetterTargetSetupUndo
DULL U' L' UU' L U L'
ELUB L' B'B L B'
FLFB L2 B'B L2 B'
HLBL' B L B'B L' B' L
JRUB' R BB' R' B
Remember: Do NOT use M, S, or E moves for setups in M2. Only use outer layers (U, L, F, R, B, D).
